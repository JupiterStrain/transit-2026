THE CLAIM

An infrared observation of this geometry exists from December 1961.

It was declassified as part of the release of the Recovery Station
Kestrel material and is item PLATE-41-OBSERVATION-NOTE in that archive:

    https://jupiterstrain.github.io/incident-1961/archive/
        telemetry-research/plate-41-observation-note/

The description, written sixty-five years ago by an observer with a
photographic plate and no idea what he was looking at:

    "Two curved traces, entering the field from opposite margins and
     converging."

    "At the point of convergence, a return of [redacted]. The observer
     notes that the figure exceeds the calibrated range of the plate and
     that the value given is a floor and not a measurement."

    "They are moving. Slowly, and towards each other, and they have been
     doing it for as long as anyone has thought to look."

Compare CVO-Q-26041-0338, this April, on an 8.2 m telescope:

    Two curved rims, entering the field from opposite margins and
    converging, with an unresolved source between them at a temperature
    the instrument was not calibrated to expect.

In April 2026 the two rims are 2.04 arcseconds apart, which is 36,800 km,
with an 1150 K source in the gap.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT WORKS: THE CONVERGENCE RETURN

Infrared emulsions of that period responded out to roughly 1.2 um and no
further. A 1150 K source has its peak at 2.52 um and puts 0.7% of its
output shortward of 1.2 um, so the observer was recording the weakest
edge of the emission with the least sensitive part of his instrument.

Even so, the numbers are not prohibitive. The hot component of the
present object, at 1.2 um, would be:

    at  5 AU     magnitude 10.8
    at 10 AU     magnitude 12.3
    at 20 AU     magnitude 13.8
    at 30 AU     magnitude 14.7

The field and the instrument on Plate 41 are both redacted, so we cannot
say which line applies. We can say that the first two are comfortable for
a hypersensitised infrared plate on a large reflector in 1961, and that
the observer's own remark - that his figure was a floor and not a
measurement, because it ran past the top of his calibration wedge - is
exactly what somebody reads off a plate when the source is well inside
his reach and well outside his scale.

His plate could not have told him a temperature. It could only tell him
that whatever he had was past the top of the scale, which is precisely
what he wrote down.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT DOES NOT WORK: THE TWO CURVED TRACES

This is the part of the note that fails, and it is put here rather than
in a footnote.

At 1.2 um a 50 K surface emits nothing that an emulsion could record.
Not faintly. The cold component of the present object puts 1e-94 Jy into
that band, which is ninety orders of magnitude below the reach of a
hypersensitised plate. Whatever the
1961 observer's two curved traces were, they were not thermal emission
from anything resembling the rims imaged this April, and this note does
not know what they were.

Three possibilities have been put to me and none of them can be tested
from a redacted document. The traces may be obscuration rather than
emission - two perfectly black structures crossing a star field leave a
trail of absences, and the object we have now is dark to better than one
part in fifty-five million (OPT-2026-K1-NULL). The 1961 pair may have been
much hotter than the present one. Or the resemblance may be a
resemblance, and nothing more, and this note may be the thing OCAC's
transmittal branch said would happen.

What is left after all of that is smaller than the claim in the title,
and it is this. A 1961 plate recorded two converging curved features with
an over-range return at the point of convergence. In April 2026 an 8.2 m
telescope recorded the same description with numbers attached.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT THIS IS NOT

It is not an archival detection of the present object. There is no source
at the present sky position in IRAS, AKARI, 2MASS, UKIDSS, VISTA or
unWISE, and the Hyperion circular says so. The object was not here
before. Plate 41 is not a picture of this position; it is a picture of
this description.

There is a catalogued outer-system variable, known since the 1960s, whose
signature is two curved sources converging with the flux between them
rising and then falling away again. It recurs every 12.78 years. That is
the Jupiter-Neptune synodic period to three figures, which is why the
source has sat in the literature for sixty years under a one-line
resonance explanation that nobody has had cause to revisit.

Plate 41 is the earliest record of it and the only one on film. There
have been five since: 1974, 1987, 2000, 2013, and one in late 2025.

The first five behaved identically. The flux rose, held, and fell, and
the two sources separated again.

The 2025 event arrived on schedule and has not fallen away. Four months
later this object was detected inbound at 30 AU, with a non-gravitational
deceleration term nothing in the catalogue prepares anybody for
(ORB-2026-K1).

This note is not claiming those are the same object. It is observing that
a source which kept perfect time for sixty-four years stopped keeping it
once, and that something left the outer system shortly afterwards.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE ANNOTATION

In grease pencil, in the corner of the 1961 plate, in the observer's own
hand:

        PLATE 41 - 1961 - NOT JUPITER

The transmittal note records that the plate was forwarded to the Kestrel
file over the objection of the office that held it, and that the
objection was overruled on the grounds that the observer's annotation
"denies a connection which nobody had proposed."

Somebody in 1961 looked at this geometry and the first thing he felt he
had to rule out, in writing, on the plate itself, was Jupiter.

Nobody has ever explained why.
